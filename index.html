Comparing different council services

This radar chart brings several service indicators together for a selected council.

READ THE CHART

Each spoke represents a different measure, such as libraries, waste, aquatic recreation or consultation. Values are scaled separately from 0 to 100.

WHAT TO NOTICE

The 2024–25 indicators measure different things: library visits, waste diversion, aquatic use and consultation satisfaction. The shape shows the selected council’s relative position on each measure. KEY FINDINGS & CONCLUSION

What the data tells us

Where services are located

Recreation facilities are not evenly distributed across Victoria. Some councils have more recorded facilities than others, while the hotspot maps show where these facilities are concentrated. This highlights differences in the availability and distribution of local services.

How services are used

Library visits, waste collection and service indicators show how council services vary across locations and over time. Some councils report higher usage or different service outcomes, showing that each community has its own needs and patterns of service use.

Looking at the bigger picture

Financial figures, library costs and consultation satisfaction add another perspective to council performance. Higher spending or larger financial totals do not necessarily mean better services. These measures need to be considered together to understand the differences between councils.
